Welcome to New Haven

Start time and follow a resident. People work, eat, rest, build relationships, remember milestones, and respond to a changing town. Click a resident in the world, or browse Citizens to choose someone to follow.

A world you can touch

Drag the map to explore. Use + and − to zoom. Pause any time to inspect a story. One day advances the whole town; +10 lets a larger chapter unfold. Faster playback changes how often days advance, not the rules of the world.

Give the council a nudge

Fund a festival, bring food relief, invest in education, change taxation, or automate work. Each action changes resources or opportunities. A storm can expose how resilient your settlement is. Read the chronicle and citizen memories to see the consequences.

People, not just percentages

Each resident has needs, personality, a goal, wealth, and a social world. Happiness and health tell different stories. Watch hunger, energy, friendships, and personal memories together to understand what a resident needs.

Rewind, replay, compare

The seed fixes the starting world and random sequence. Save downloads a snapshot you can load later. Creating a new world replaces the current session, so save first if you want to keep its story. Experiments run independent paired worlds and report differences across multiple seeds.

Where AI fits

The town runs without an AI service or token cost. If enabled by the owner, important events may request bounded decisions from an AI provider; ordinary activities remain rules-based. AI-enabled runs are not guaranteed to replay identically. Never enter API keys into a world save or this page.

What this world can tell you

New Haven is a model: its outcomes follow designed rules and assumptions. Try hypotheses, inspect outcomes, and look for surprising stories. Experiments are not validated predictions of real-world economics, politics, or human behavior.
