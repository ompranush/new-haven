AI settings

Your API key is sent to this app's server session over the connection. It is never returned in world snapshots, included in save files, or stored in browser local storage. Session expiry removes it.

Availability depends on your API account. All AI features use this model. Reasoning models may take longer and consume more tokens; no model is called just by saving settings.

The limit counts failed attempts too and is shared by God Mode, citizen reflection and research. It is a call limit, not a currency budget. Ordinary Play never calls a model. Never paste secrets into God Mode commands.

Welcome to New Haven

Start time and follow a resident. People work, eat, rest, build relationships, remember milestones, and respond to a changing town. Click a resident in the world, or browse Citizens to choose someone to follow.

A world you can touch

Drag the map to explore. Use + and − to zoom. Pause any time to inspect a story. One day advances the whole town; +10 lets a larger chapter unfold. Faster playback changes how often days advance, not the rules of the world.

Give the council a nudge

Fund a festival, bring food relief, invest in education, change taxation, or automate work. Each action changes resources or opportunities. A storm can expose how resilient your settlement is. Read the chronicle and citizen memories to see the consequences.

People, not just percentages

Each resident has needs, personality, a goal, wealth, and a social world. Happiness and health tell different stories. Watch hunger, energy, friendships, and personal memories together to understand what a resident needs.

Rewind, replay, compare

The seed fixes the starting world and random sequence. Save downloads a snapshot you can load later. Creating a new world replaces the current session, so save first if you want to keep its story. Experiments run independent paired worlds and report differences across multiple seeds.

Is this really AI?

By default this is an agent-based simulation. Citizens respond to needs and events using programmed rules, personalities and memories. These decisions are labelled RULES. This is not a society of autonomous language models.

Optional AI sends one important event and citizen context to a language model. It chooses a permitted action and explains why. Actual model decisions are labelled AI in the decision history. Enable it with your own key in Settings. Only explicit God Mode interpretation, reflection requests or AI experiments spend tokens; ordinary Play remains rules-only.

Consequences take time

Floods destroy food and damage workplaces. Repairs need workers, money and materials. Food, trade and repair flows appear in the daily resource ledger. Relief is a limited emergency purchase, not an unlimited grant. Upgrades and festivals have costs and cooldowns.

Scenery versus simulation

Hills and decorative vegetation establish the landscape. Citizens, workplace condition, crisis effects and resource changes come from the simulation. Ambient animation does not advance simulated time. Save before closing your browser: this world does not run offline.

What this world can tell you

New Haven is a model: its outcomes follow designed rules and assumptions. Try hypotheses, inspect outcomes, and look for surprising stories. Experiments are not validated predictions of real-world economics, politics, or human behavior.
